## Design

`award_norm_key(funder_id, award_id, side)` (generated by `scripts/gen_award_norm_key.py`
from the vendored rules in `scripts/award_translation_rules.py` — 17 validated funders):
per-funder translation with the generic 07-31 key as byte-identical fallback. All key sites
in CreateAwards + WorkAwards route through it. Two-key matching: generic first (shipped
behavior preserved), sharp adds.

`award_id_is_weak(funder_id, award_id)` (same generated file): TRUE when a deposited
string's normalized form is a bare number at a dense-numeric-registry funder (CIHR, Wellcome,
SNSF, EC, NSF, NSERC — chosen by measured foreign-hit rates 15–40%). Weak forms contribute
NO sharp key anywhere (aliases, collapse, works consumers, alias-derived keys) and verdict
`confirmed_weak` (kept + flagged, never auto-merged).

**FAMILY MERGE: ON** (@kyle approved 2026-08-03): a sharp key hitting >1 registry award
(NIH renewal-year lineages) merges deposits onto the NEWEST year's record (end_year DESC
election, consistent across aliases/collapse/works). NSERC is exempt by construction — its
keys are year-qualified because NSERC reuses serials across years for different grants.
